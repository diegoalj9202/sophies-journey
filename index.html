Teacher Guide

Sophie's Journey follows Sophie from The Wanderer as she sails across the Atlantic to meet her grandfather, Bompie. Each picture has 3 levels. In every level, students get words or a hint first, say their own sentence, and then reveal a model sentence that uses exactly those words.

  • Level 1 · Simple – 4 key words: 1 action verb + 3 nouns.
  • Level 2 · Compound – + 2 new words: a FANBOYS word and one more word.
  • Level 3 · Complex – a hint (joining word + idea) and a sentence frame with blanks.

Student steps

  1. Look at the picture.
  2. Click “Reveal words” and say a simple sentence with all of them.
  3. Click “Show sentence” and compare.
  4. Click “Level up 🔵”: add the new words to make a compound sentence. Then show the sentence and compare.
  5. Click “Level up 🟠”: use the hint and the frame to make a complex sentence. Then show the sentence and compare.
  6. Check the map: How far is Sophie now? How many km are left?

Only the next button appears, so the class moves step by step. In each model sentence the target words are highlighted in the same colour as their chips, and the chips get a ✓ when they are used. Click any chip or 🔊 to hear it. Re-test hides everything again.

Joining words (reference)

  • FANBOYS (comma before them): and (add) · but, yet (different idea) · so (result) · or (choice) · for (reason) · nor
  • Complex (comma after the first part): time when, while, before, after, as soon as, until · reason because, since · contrast although, even though · condition if, unless

Edit the content (PICTURES_DATA)

Open script.js. At the top you will find the PICTURES_DATA list. Each picture looks like this: 

{
  id: 6,
  title: "Dolphins at sunset",
  image: "images/scene-06.jpg",
  alt: "Sophie steering the boat at sunset with dolphins.",
  km: 1500,
  levels: {
    simple: {
      words: ["steer", "boat", "dolphins", "sunset"], // verb first
      sentence: "Sophie steers the boat near the dolphins at sunset."
    },
    compound: {
      newWords: ["and", "jump"],  // FANBOYS word + 1 word
      sentence: "Sophie steers the boat near the dolphins at sunset, and they jump next to her."
    },
    complex: {
      conjunction: "when",
      idea: "she sees them",
      frame: "When ___, Sophie ___.",  // ___ = blank
      sentence: "When Sophie sees the dolphins, she steers the boat closer at sunset."
    }
  }
}

Change any text between the quotes and save. Use the target words in the sentence: the app highlights them automatically, also when the verb changes (read → reads, carry → carries, run → running). The joining word is underlined automatically. km moves the boat on the map (0 to 5,500).

Replace the images

Put your pictures in the images folder with the same names: scene-01.jpg … scene-10.jpg. Use landscape pictures (16:9). If a picture is missing, the app shows a coloured placeholder with the scene number and title.

Keyboard: Esc = Home  ·  ← / → = previous / next picture.
