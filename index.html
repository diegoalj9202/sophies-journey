Teacher Guide

Sophie's Journey follows Sophie from The Wanderer as she sails across the Atlantic to meet her grandfather, Bompie. Students describe each picture and practise three types of sentences:

  • Simple – one idea. Sophie steers the boat.
  • Compound – two ideas joined with FANBOYS (for, and, nor, but, or, yet, so).
  • Complex – an idea with because, when, although, while, before, after, as soon as.

Student steps

  1. Look at the picture and say what is happening.
  2. Click “Reveal words” and check your key words. The action word is the verb.
  3. Click “Reveal phrase” to see the sentences level by level (🟢 → 🔵 → 🟠). Try to say the harder sentence before you click “Level up”.
  4. Look at the map: How far is Sophie now? How many km are left?

Press 🔊 on any sentence card to hear it read aloud.

Edit the content (PICTURES_DATA)

Open script.js. At the top you will find the PICTURES_DATA list. Each picture looks like this: 

{
  id: 6,
  title: "Dolphins at sunset",
  image: "images/scene-06.jpg",
  alt: "Sophie steering the boat at sunset with dolphins.",
  words: ["steer", "wheel", "dolphins", "sunset"], // verb first
  km: 1500,
  phrases: {
    simple:   "Sophie steers the boat.",
    compound: "The sun is going down, and dolphins are jumping.",
    complex:  "When Sophie sees the dolphins, she smiles."
  }
}

Change any text between the quotes and save. The joining word (and, but, so, when, because…) is underlined automatically. km moves the boat on the map (0 to 5,500).

Replace the images

Put your pictures in the images folder with the same names: scene-01.jpg … scene-10.jpg. Use landscape pictures (16:9). If a picture is missing, the app shows a coloured placeholder with the scene number and title.

Keyboard: Esc = Home  ·  ← / → = previous / next picture.
